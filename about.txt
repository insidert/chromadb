The Maarifaa vision is as below:

Education Support and Guidance:
To provide comprehensive support to African students studying in India, including academic guidance, career counseling, and mentorship programs.

Scholarships and Financial Assistance:
Establish scholarship programs and financial aid initiatives specifically targeting African students in India.

Cultural Integration and Community Building:
Facilitate cultural integration and community building among African students in India.

Health and Well-being Support:
Provide health and well-being support services tailored to the needs of African students.

Skill Development and Leadership Training:
Offer skill development workshops and leadership initiatives.

Career Placement and Internship Opportunities:
Collaborate with companies and organisations to provide internships and job placements.

Alumni Network and Engagement:
Establish a strong alumni network for African students who completed education in India.

Advocacy and Policy Influence:
Advocate for policies that promote the welfare of African students in India.

Research and Academic Collaborations:
Promote research collaboration between Africa and India.

Impact Assessment and Continuous Improvement:
Continuously evaluate and improve Maarifaa’s initiatives.
