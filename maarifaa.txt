The maarifaa vision is as below: Education Support and Guidance: To provide comprehensive support to African students studying in India, including academic guidance, career counseling, and mentorship programs. Help students navigate the Indian education system, access resources, and develop the necessary skills for success.
Scholarships and Financial Assistance: Establish scholarship programs and financial aid initiatives specifically targeting African students in India. Provide financial support to deserving students who face financial constraints, enabling them to pursue higher education and achieve their academic goals.
Cultural Integration and Community Building: Facilitate cultural integration and community building among African students in India. Organise cultural events, celebrations, and networking opportunities that promote cross-cultural understanding, friendship, and a sense of belonging.
Health and Well-being Support: Provide health and well-being support services tailored to the needs of African students. This can include access to healthcare facilities, mental health resources, awareness campaigns, and counseling services to ensure their overall well-being.
Skill Development and Leadership Training: Offer skill development workshops, training programs, and leadership initiatives that equip African students with essential skills for personal and professional growth. Provide opportunities for them to enhance their employability and leadership potential.
Career Placement and Internship Opportunities: Collaborate with companies, organisations, and industry partners to facilitate career placement and internship opportunities for African students. Establish connections with potential employers and offer assistance in securing internships and job placements after graduation.
Alumni Network and Engagement: Establish a strong alumni network for African students who have completed their education in India. Foster ongoing engagement, mentoring, and professional networking opportunities to support their post-graduation journey and contribute to the growth of the Maarifaa community.
Advocacy and Policy Influence: Advocate for policies and initiatives that promote the rights and welfare of African students in India. Engage with relevant stakeholders, educational institutions, and government bodies to address any challenges or barriers they may face and ensure a conducive learning environment.
Research and Academic Collaborations: Facilitate research collaborations between African students in India and academic institutions in Africa. Promote knowledge exchange, joint research projects, and academic partnerships that contribute to the development of both regions.
Impact Assessment and Continuous Improvement: Implement robust monitoring and evaluation mechanisms to assess the impact of Maarifaa’s programs and initiatives for African students. Continuously gather feedback, measure outcomes, and make data-driven decisions to improve and enhance the services provided.
