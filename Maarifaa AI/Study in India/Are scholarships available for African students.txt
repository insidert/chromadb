Are scholarships available for African students?Yes.Indian Universities (Maarifaa Partner Universities):All universities partnered with Maarifaa offer guaranteed partial scholarships to international students, covering a significant portion of tuition fees. These scholarships are available directly through the courses listed on the Maarifaa dashboard and do not require waiting for government timelines.Government of India Scholarships:Including the Indian Council for Cultural Relations (ICCR) and other government-supported schemes, which are competitive and quota-based.
